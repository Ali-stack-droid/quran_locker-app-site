Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026
Applies to the QuranLocker mobile application (com.quran.locker.muslim.app)

The short version. QuranLocker does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your personal information, and does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking software of any kind. We operate no servers and we receive no data about you. Everything the app remembers — your settings, your reading progress, your bookmarks — is stored only on your own device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

This policy explains what QuranLocker does and does not do with information, in plain language. It applies to the QuranLocker application for Android and iOS ("the app"). The app is published by [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"), based in Pakistan.

1. Information we do not collect

We want to be specific rather than vague, because "we value your privacy" means nothing on its own. QuranLocker does not collect, request, receive or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or date of birth
  • Any account or login credentials — the app has no accounts and no sign-in
  • Your GPS or precise location. The app does not request location permission at all; prayer times are calculated from a city that you select from a list included in the app
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, call logs or SMS messages
  • Your advertising identifier, device identifier or any other tracking identifier
  • Analytics, usage statistics, behavioural data, heat maps or session recordings
  • Crash reports containing personal information

QuranLocker contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no social-media SDK and no attribution or tracking SDK. We do not sell, rent, share or trade information about you, because we do not have any.

2. Information stored on your device

So that the app is useful between sessions, it saves certain things in your device's own private app storage. This information stays on your device. It is never uploaded to us, and we have no way of reading it.

  • Your preferences — selected city, prayer calculation method, madhab, high latitude rule, reciter, translation and tafseer choices, font size, theme, and language
  • Your reading activity — last position read, bookmarks, reading streaks and session history used to show your own progress inside the app
  • Your learning progress — quiz answers, scores, milestones and deck progress
  • Notification settings — which prayer reminders you have enabled and their timing
  • Downloaded content — Quran text, translations, tafseer and recitation audio you have chosen to save for offline use

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes it completely.

3. Permissions the app requests, and why

Permission Why QuranLocker needs it
INTERNET To download Quran text, translations, tafseer and recitation audio from the public sources listed in section 4. The app works offline once content is downloaded.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show prayer time reminders. Notifications are created and scheduled entirely on your device. There is no push server and no message is ever sent to your device from us.
USE_EXACT_ALARM
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM		 Prayer reminders must arrive at an exact minute to be useful. Android requires this permission for alarms that are not delayed by battery optimisation.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To re-schedule your prayer reminders after your device restarts. Without it, reminders would silently stop working after every reboot.
SET_WALLPAPER For the lock screen ayah feature, which sets an image containing a verse as your wallpaper when you ask it to. The app does not read your existing wallpaper.

QuranLocker requests no other permissions. In particular it requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage-read permission.

4. Third-party content sources

QuranLocker does not host Quran text or recitation audio itself. When you open a surah, change a translation, or play a recitation that is not already downloaded, your device requests that content directly from these publicly available services:

  • Al Quran Cloud — api.alquran.cloud (Quran text and translations)
  • Quran.com API — api.quran.com (tafseer)
  • Islamic Network CDN — cdn.islamic.network (recitation audio)

Because your device connects to these services directly, they will see your device's IP address and standard technical request information, in the same way any website you visit does. This is a normal consequence of downloading content over the internet. We do not control these services and we receive no copy of these requests. We send them no information about you — no identifier, no account, no profile — only the reference for the passage or audio file being requested.

If you wish to avoid these connections entirely, download the content you want once and then use the app offline.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

If QuranLocker offers a paid subscription or in-app purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by the app store you bought it from — Google Play on Android, or the Apple App Store on iOS.

  • Payment is processed by the store. We never see, receive or store your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • We use RevenueCat as a subscription management service, to confirm whether a purchase is valid and still active. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from the store and a randomly generated, anonymous identifier for your installation of the app. It does not receive your name, email address or payment details from us.
  • Your subscription is managed, renewed and cancelled through your Google Play or Apple App Store account, not through us. Refunds are handled under the store's refund policy.

For details of how these companies handle your data, see the Google Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Children's privacy

QuranLocker is suitable for users of all ages and is safe for children to use. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children. We do not knowingly gather data from children under 13, and there is no mechanism in the app by which a child could disclose personal information to us.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. There is no account to delete and no server-side record of you to request the removal of.

Information stored locally on your device remains there until you remove it. To delete all of it:

  • Android — Settings → Apps → QuranLocker → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app
  • iOS — press and hold the app icon and choose Delete App

8. Security

All network requests made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections. Data saved by the app is held in your operating system's private per-application storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read. Because no personal data ever leaves your device, there is no central database of user information that could be breached.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data held about you, and to object to its processing. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase. The data the app stores is under your direct control on your own device at all times. You are welcome to contact us using the details below if you have any question about this.
